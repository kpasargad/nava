# Brand Context

## Brand
- **Name**: نوا / Nava
- **Category**: Windows voice-to-text companion.
- **Description**: Hold a shortcut, speak Persian or English, and see your words become text as you speak.
- **Stage**: Working personal desktop product with a private GitHub release.
- **Website**: No public marketing site specified. The private repository is the distribution channel.

## Audience
- **Primary audience**: People who want to capture thoughts, draft text, or transcribe conversations without interrupting their desktop work.
- **Key problem**: Typing and managing recording tools interrupt the thought they want to capture.
- **Their language**: «نگه دار و صحبت کن»، «متن زنده»، «یادداشت»، «میانبر»، «گوینده».
- **Language**: Persian interface; Persian and English speech. Keep the interface Persian unless localization is separately requested.

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: True push-to-talk, visible live text, a tiny optional character, quiet cues, and a user-configured transcription service.
- **Reference**: The user explicitly referenced Soniox's live transcription experience. Other competitors are not established.
- **Market position**: A personal productivity companion. Pricing and commercial positioning are unspecified.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality words**: Calm, attentive, clear, warm, lightly playful.
- **Tone**: Natural written Persian with singular, friendly instructions. Characterful without baby talk or constant jokes.
- **Voice admires**: No verbal identity reference specified; derive voice from the approved quiet, unobtrusive product experience.

## Values & Mission
- **Core values**: Low interruption, user control, honest feedback, clear next steps, respect for personal information.
- **Mission**: Make it easy to capture what you want to say, then return attention to your work.
- **Constraints**: Nava sends audio to the configured service. Do not describe transcription as offline or promise that all audio stays on the computer.

## Goals
- **Primary goal**: A comfortable, reliable daily tool with a consistent human voice.
- **Working quality measures**: Users understand hold/release, know whether audio is being captured, can find their text, and know what to do after an error.
- **Commercial targets**: Not specified; no revenue or growth claims are assumed.

This context combines explicit product decisions from the conversation with the audience working assumption above. Update it when product direction changes. See [the voice guide](../docs/VOICE.md) for writing rules.
